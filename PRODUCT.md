# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

- Potential clients looking for website, web-service, or application development.
- Employers and technical leads evaluating Kirill's experience, engineering judgment, and specific project contribution.

## Product Purpose

K-Studio is Kirill Kovalenko's portfolio and work platform. It presents projects and learning materials, accepts project briefs, and provides a direct route to discuss work or collaboration. Success means visitors can understand the work and Kirill's role, trust the evidence shown, and contact him through Telegram or email.

## Positioning

Project cases distinguish what a product is from what Kirill personally built. The portfolio separates his own product work, client projects, participation in other projects, and archived projects whose role or contribution is not currently published as verified.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse the public portfolio on desktop or mobile, inspect case details, and contact Kirill. Clients may use the project brief form. Employers and technical leads use case descriptions, responsibilities, technical decisions, and stack to assess experience. Content is managed through authenticated administrative pages; those pages are outside the public portfolio audit scope unless a public flow depends on them.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Public routes include the home page, project portfolio, contact page, materials, tutorials, and UI components.
- Project cases are loaded through a public API and persisted project data; the portfolio categorizes them as OWN, CLIENT, PARTICIPATION, or UNVERIFIED.
- Contact options include Telegram, email, GitHub, and an on-site project brief form.
- The application uses Nuxt 3, Vue 3, TypeScript, Nitro APIs, PostgreSQL, and Prisma.
- Do not invent outcomes, statistics, customer endorsements, or project claims that are not supported by repository data or user confirmation.
- This request is documentation and evaluation only: no redesign, business-logic changes, production changes, push, or deployment.

## Brand Commitments

The product is named K-Studio and is a personal portfolio/work platform for Kirill Kovalenko. For this evaluation, preserve the existing portfolio identity: green accent, light and dark themes, clean typography, compact navigation, restrained project cards, and modern modal presentation. The user selected comp-first for future new interfaces; small fixes to existing components may use code-first.

## Evidence on Hand

- Project records and case-study fields are implemented in the repository and backed by the public projects API.
- Public case content includes project summaries, role, responsibilities, technical highlights, technologies, and verified links where available.
- Four local project preview assets exist under `public/images/projects/`: K-Studio, KES, MALINA, and OKNA WebP images.
- No testimonials, independently verified business outcomes, or quantified success metrics were provided for this task; do not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Make the developer's actual responsibility explicit.
- Separate product context from personal contribution.
- Keep unverified claims out of public case content.
- Make a relevant contact path easy to find.
- Preserve the established portfolio identity while improving clarity and usability.
